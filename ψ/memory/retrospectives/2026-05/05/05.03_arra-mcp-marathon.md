## Lessons Learned

**Stacking PRs scales when the unit of work is small enough.** 10 PRs over 30 hours, each ~150-500 LOC, each with tests, each independently reviewable — that's a different operating mode than mega-PRs. The cost is review-context overhead per PR. The benefit is catastrophic-failure isolation and merge-time predictability. Worth the trade for projects with active maintainers.

**Tests are the enabler for stacking, not just the verifier.** The 70 hermetic tests across the indexer M-chain are why I could ship 5 PRs that depend on each other in 90 minutes without breaking. Each PR's tests defended its own contract. The next PR could rely on prior contracts staying stable. That's the actual mechanism behind "stacked PRs work."

**Don't claim production lift without a production eval.** Smoke tests show CAPABILITY ceilings; production tests show production behavior. Both are true; neither is sufficient alone. The +14.3 pts smoke / +0 production reality is the right kind of disagreement — neither erased the other.

**Negative results are valuable.** SEA-LION was the candidate that should have unseated bge-m3 per public benchmarks. We tested empirically; it lost by 36 points on our use case. That's a more useful finding than a positive lift would have been — it stops a future migration that would have wasted a week.
